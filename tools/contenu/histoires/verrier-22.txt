--- EN
On Wednesday, a beautiful envelope appeared on Inès's desk. Inside, there was a card in elegant handwriting.

"Dear Inès, my wife and I would be delighted if you could join us for dinner on Thursday at eight. A small thank-you for your hard work on the Verrier account. Warm regards, Richard Hale."

She wanted to throw it in the bin. Instead, she called Helen.

"Go," said Helen. "Refusing would look suspicious. Be polite, eat, say nothing important, and leave early. And send me a message when you are home."

The Hales lived in a large white house in a quiet street in Richmond, with a garden and two cars in front. Susan Hale opened the door. She was blonde, friendly and wore a lot of jewelry.

The dinner was excellent. Richard was charming. He talked about wine, about his trips to France, about his daughter at university. He asked Inès about her family, her studies, her plans. He was a perfect host.

Then, at dessert, Susan went to the kitchen to make coffee, and Richard poured more wine into Inès's glass.

"You know, Inès, after the merger, Atlas will need a new head of client accounts for Europe. Daniel is tired. He's not ambitious anymore. But you…" He smiled. "You are young, bilingual and very determined. I could recommend you. My recommendation would count."

"That's kind of you."

"It's not kindness. It's business. In business, loyalty is the most important quality. People who are loyal are rewarded. People who are not…" He put the bottle down very gently. "Well. They usually have a very short career. In London, everybody knows everybody."

Susan came back with the coffee and a big smile. Inès drank it quickly and said she had an early meeting.

In the taxi, she sent Helen a message: "Home soon. He offered me Daniel's job. And he threatened me. Politely."

As the taxi turned the corner, she looked back. Behind them, a silver sports car was starting its engine.

« Allez-y, a dit Helen. Refuser paraîtrait suspect. Soyez polie, mangez, ne dites rien d'important et partez tôt. Et envoyez-moi un message quand vous serez rentrée. »
