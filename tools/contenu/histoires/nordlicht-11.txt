--- EN
They met after work in a small Turkish café across the street. It was warm inside, and it smelled of tea and sugar. Camille showed Aylin the messages from Pavel.

Aylin read them slowly. Then she put the phone on the table and looked at her hands.

"I've known Jonas for nine years," she said. "We studied together in Munich. When I built the first version of Shiftly, he was the only person who believed in it. He found our first clients. He found our first investors. Without him, there is no Nordlicht."

"I understand," said Camille softly.

"But he always talked about the exit," Aylin continued. "Selling the company, making a lot of money, starting something new. I never wanted that. I wanted to build something good and keep building it."

"Do you think he wants to sell Nordlicht to Planwise?"

Aylin did not answer immediately. "Maybe. Last autumn, he had many meetings in Amsterdam. He said they were with investors."

The waiter brought more tea. They stayed silent until he left.

"We don't know everything yet," said Camille. "Maybe there's an explanation. Maybe Jonas wanted to show Planwise our numbers for a partnership, and he made a stupid mistake."

"Giving a competitor the names of our clients' staff is not a small mistake," said Aylin. "It's illegal. These are real people. Mia, Tobias, Sara. They never agreed to that."

Camille nodded. "So what do we do?"

Aylin took a long breath. "First, we collect facts. Dates, messages, logs. Not opinions. Then we decide."

"And Jonas?"

"Jonas is my friend," said Aylin. Her voice was quiet but firm. "But our clients trusted me with their data. I built the system. If someone used it badly, it is my responsibility too."

They paid and stepped outside. The snow had turned to rain. Across the street, the light in the meeting room was still on. Jonas was still working.
