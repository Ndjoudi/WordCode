--- EN
Camille did not sleep well that night. She lay in bed and listened to the trams in the street. She thought about Lukas and Mia, and about the names on the giant screen.

In the morning, she arrived early. Jonas was already there, which was unusual. He was standing in the kitchen with a coffee, looking at his phone.

"Good morning," he said. "You look tired."

"I am," said Camille. Then she decided to be direct. "Jonas, about the Planwise demo. I checked. Lukas never used Planwise. The data is ours. It came from the old test server."

Jonas put his phone in his pocket. For a moment, he said nothing.

"The test server," he repeated. "Is it still online?"

"Yes. Aylin thinks someone downloaded a file in December."

Jonas walked to the window. "Okay. Listen to me carefully. This is serious, and we will deal with it. But not now."

"Not now?"

"We launch in four weeks. Margaret Hale arrives in three weeks. If we start a big investigation now, the team will panic, the launch will fail and the investment will disappear. Then there is no Nordlicht, and nobody can protect anybody's data."

"But our clients—"

"Our clients are fine," said Jonas. "Planwise used some names in a demo. That's ugly, but nobody lost money. Nobody got hurt. Aylin will shut down the test server today. After the launch, we'll look at everything calmly."

Camille felt that something was wrong with this logic, but she could not say exactly what. Jonas was the CEO. He knew the investors, the lawyers, the history of the company. She had been in Berlin for six weeks.

"Okay," she said slowly. "After the launch."

"Thank you," said Jonas. He smiled, but his eyes did not smile. "And Camille? Let's keep this between us. And Aylin. No need to worry the others."

When he left, Camille went to Aylin's desk. Aylin did not look up.

"He told you to wait," said Aylin.

"How do you know?"

"Because he told me the same thing ten minutes before you arrived."

« Bonjour, a-t-il dit. Tu as l'air fatiguée.
