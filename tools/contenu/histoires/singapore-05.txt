--- EN
For two weeks, the team worked in the meeting room at Harbourline, surrounded by boxes of documents.

Her job was the receivables. Receivables are money that customers owe a company for goods or services already delivered, but not yet paid. At the end of the year, customers owed Harbourline three hundred and ten million dollars.

To check receivables, auditors send letters to customers. The letter says: "Our records show you owe Harbourline this amount. Is that correct?" The customer must reply directly to the auditors, not to the company.

Nadia chose a sample of forty customers. Most were large, well-known companies: a Japanese electronics firm, an Indian steel company, a Chinese car manufacturer. One by one, their answers came back. Yes, correct. Yes, correct.

Then she looked at the biggest customer of all.

"Orchid Star Trading Limited. Labuan, Malaysia." Amount owed: ninety-six million dollars. Almost one third of all receivables.

"I've never heard of them," said Nadia.

"Neither have I," said Mei Lin. "But that doesn't mean anything. Asia is full of big companies nobody has heard of."

Nadia sent the confirmation letter. The answer came back in two days. Very fast. It arrived by email, with a signed PDF attached. "We confirm the balance of USD 96,000,000 owed to Harbourline Logistics. Signed: Kenneth Wong, Director."

"Perfect," said Mei Lin. "Done."

But Arjun was looking at his screen with a strange expression.

"Mei Lin," he said slowly. "The email came from orchidstar-trading.com. I checked the domain name. Out of curiosity."

"And?"

"It was registered twenty-three days ago." He turned his laptop towards them. "A company that owes ninety-six million dollars. And its website and email address were created three weeks ago. The day after we sent our confirmation letters."

Nobody spoke. The air conditioning hummed.

Mei Lin looked at the screen for a long time. Then she closed the door of the meeting room.

"Okay," she said very quietly. "Professional scepticism. Arjun, find out everything you can about Orchid Star. Nadia, find their address in Labuan. Nobody talks about this outside this room. Not yet."
