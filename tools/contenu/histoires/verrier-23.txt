--- EN
The silver car followed the taxi for ten minutes. Then, at a traffic light, it turned left and disappeared. Inès did not sleep much that night.

On Friday morning, she called the unknown number that had sent her the message about the October invoices. Nobody answered. Then, five minutes later, a new message arrived.

"Not on the phone. Dartford. Canteen. 12.30."

At half past twelve, the canteen of the Dartford warehouse was full of workers in yellow vests eating sausages and chips. In a corner, alone at a table, sat Ray Collins.

"Hello, love," he said. "Sit down. Do you want a tea? The coffee here is terrible."

"It was you," said Inès. "The message."

"It was me." He pushed a cup of tea towards her. "I'm sixty-two. I've worked here for thirty-one years. I've seen a lot of managers come and go. Most of them never asked me anything. You came down here in your city shoes and asked how things work. I liked that."

He took an old notebook out of his jacket. The cover was dirty and the corners were folded.

"I've had a feeling about Swift Freight for a long time. Their trucks arrived empty and left too fast. The drivers never talked. So, every time one of their trucks took Verrier boxes, I wrote it down. The date, the time, the number of boxes, the name of the driver. On paper. Paper can't be deleted."

Inès opened the notebook. There were pages and pages of small, careful handwriting. More than forty trucks. Two years of notes.

"Ray, this is…"

"It's what I could do." He shrugged. "I'm an old man. I can't go to Essex and take photos. But I can write."

"Why didn't you show this to anyone before?"

"I did. I showed it to someone in the finance department, last year." He looked at his tea. "He told me to mind my own business. A week later, my overtime was cancelled."

"Who was it?"

Ray looked up.

"Who do you think?"

« Salut, ma petite, a-t-il dit. Assieds-toi. Tu veux un thé ? Le café ici est horrible.

Inès a ouvert le carnet. Il y avait des pages et des pages d'une petite écriture soignée. Plus de quarante camions. Deux ans de notes.
